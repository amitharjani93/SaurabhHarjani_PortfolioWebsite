Property matters turn on documents. Whether the question is a purchase, a lease,
an inheritance or a dispute with a builder, the answer is almost always contained
in the instruments already signed and registered — and in the ones that should
have been but were not.

The work here begins with reading those documents in order. That review produces
a plain account of what has been established and what has not, which is the basis
for everything that follows: whether to proceed with a transaction, what to insist
on before signing, and whether a dispute is worth pursuing.

Where a transaction is still open, the aim is to close the gaps before money moves.
Where a dispute has already arisen, the aim is to be accurate about the position
before deciding how hard to press it.
